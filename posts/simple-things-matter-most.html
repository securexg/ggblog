Simple Things Matter Most: Strength, Meditation, and Cutting Out Reels

I stopped using Instagram for repeated content. Everything worth keeping comes down to three simple truths: build muscle, calm the mind, and protect your attention.

Talking about simple things matters the most. That is why I stopped using Instagram for repeated, recycled content. People try to overcomplicate everything, when the real fundamentals have not changed.

1. Health: Strength Training Is Non-Negotiable

Health is the most important part of your life, and strength training is unavoidable. Muscle is something you need for the long term, and it is non-negotiable for any gender or any age group.

Cardio and walking are not bad, but cardio cannot replace strength training. I feel for people who claim walking is enough or spend an hour on cardio alone because of old-age misconceptions. Everyone has their own choice, but I am happy sticking to the simplest truth: lift weights, preferably at a nearby gym where you feel the energy and connection around you.

2. Diet: 80% Food, Clear Out the Protein Myths

Fitness is roughly 80% diet and 20% exercise. You can only train for 30 to 40 minutes, or at most an hour a day, but your diet runs all day long. Drink more water, cut down excess carbs, and keep meals minimal and healthy.

There are too many protein myths floating around that need to be cleared up:

Commonly Called "Protein" What It Actually Is Better Approach
Roti & Rice Primarily carbs Pair with real protein sources
Sattu & Oats Carb-heavy / moderate Do not rely on them as main protein
Peanut Butter Mostly fats & calories Use sparingly; add whey or sorted veg protein

Outside food is packed with sugar and cheap oils hidden behind colorful packaging. I am not saying you can never have cold drinks, pizza, chips, momos, or Maggi. Just take them in the right proportion, knowing they are for fun, not a healthy meal.

3. The Mind: Meditation Is the Only Way Out

No one in this world can avoid stress. It comes from relationships, finances (often the biggest one), or health, because no one's life is perfect.

When the mind gets worn down, meditation is the only real way out. Once you go beyond the intellect, you understand yourself. Ancient traditions have told us to go deep inside, yet people often get stuck in outer rituals, damaging rivers and the environment while ignoring their own breath and mind.

Clear the glitter out and focus on what matters. Personally, I practice meditation through courses inspired by Gurudev Sri Sri Ravi Shankar. There are many paths available, so pick one and practice it consistently. You cannot eliminate stress completely, but meditation helps you navigate it with a calmer mind.

4. Protect Both: Block Short-Form Reels

Both your body and mind get damaged by one modern trap: short-form reels are engineered to hook you. They are structured to steal hours out of your day, and willpower alone rarely beats them.

I use a free Android app called ScrollLess to block short videos and set timers (there are similar blockers on iOS too). It is not foolproof, but setting blockers keeps you reminded that endless short videos are not the answer.

Aim for 70%, Not Perfection

You do not have to be 100% strict. Aim for 60% to 80% consistency. Some days you will eat junk food, miss a workout, or skip meditation, and that is completely normal. Just stay aware of the basic truth: the body needs strength and protein, the mind needs meditation, and your attention needs protection.
